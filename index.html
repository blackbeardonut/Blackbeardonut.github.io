what we believe

What Makes Our Donuts Different

  • 👐

    Handmade Before Sunrise

    Our day starts in the dark — dough, filling and glaze are all made by hand, from scratch, every single morning.

  • ⚖️

    Not Too Sweet

    Korean dessert lovers care about one thing above all: sweetness that doesn't overwhelm. We hold back the sugar so every bite stays balanced, never sickly-sweet.

  • 😋

    A Sweetness You'll Never Get Tired Of

    Balanced doesn't mean bland. Our filling is rich enough to crave, light enough to finish the whole box without feeling weighed down.

  • 💥

    We're Rewriting What a Donut Can Be

    Forget the greasy, mass-produced donut you know. Ours is handmade, filling-forward, and made to be taken seriously as a dessert — not just a quick bite.

  • 💬

    Your Feedback Comes First

    If something isn't quite right, just tell us — anytime. Listening to our customers matters more to us than anything else.

since 2019

The bear by the beach

Black Bear Donut began in 2019 as a first-generation handmade-donut brand. Here by Gwangalli, the routine is the same as on day one: the lights come on before sunrise, the dough is mixed by hand, and whatever we make today is sold today.
